Skill directory

19 skills. One operating layer.

Every skill is a plain folder — SKILL.md plus optional scripts/, references/ and assets/. Pick a lane below, or install the whole box.

  • Core router

    opskeep

    Routes business work, Opskeep setup, and Opskeep utilities into the right skill with clear next actions, owners, dates, and unknowns.

  • Business lane

    opskeep-get-work

    Helps service businesses build and qualify a pipeline from content, inbound, referrals, outreach, and market signals.

  • Business lane

    opskeep-define-work

    Turns messy demand into defined work: discovery, scope, proposals, acceptance checks, and client onboarding.

  • Business lane

    opskeep-deliver-work

    Coordinates active delivery: status, blockers, risks, handoffs, dependencies, QA, and weekly updates.

  • Business lane

    opskeep-get-paid

    Tracks money follow-through: invoices, payments, billable time, budgets, and change-control money impact.

  • Business lane

    opskeep-keep-clients

    Maintains client trust through follow-ups, check-ins, health checks, renewals, referrals, and testimonials.

  • Business lane

    opskeep-improve-operations

    Turns finished work into better process: retrospectives, lessons, SOPs, templates, and reusable playbooks.

  • Meta

    opskeep-manage

    Onboards and manages Opskeep itself: business profile, preferences, memory, connectors, automations, triggers, and recurring reminders.

  • Meta

    opskeep-tools

    Routes standalone utilities: audio briefs, voice huddles, follow-up reminders, time tracking, and Composio-backed tool access.

  • Breakout

    opskeep-audio-brief

    Turns sessions, PRs, plans, specs, docs, URLs, and pasted markdown into a listenable brief with a shareable listening page.

  • Breakout

    opskeep-huddle-beta

    Starts a voice huddle with Opskeep for live talk-throughs, planning conversation, and concise context handoff. Needs its own deployed voice-relay backend.

  • Breakout

    composio-mcp

    Routes external app work through the Composio MCP with tool discovery, authorization links, schema-safe execution, and concise provenance.

  • Breakout

    opskeep-follow-up-reminders

    Creates and cancels deterministic one-shot email follow-up reminders, only when the user wants a specific future reminder.

  • Breakout

    opskeep-time-tracking

    Starts, stops, switches, backfills, updates, archives, and summarizes time entries.

  • Breakout

    opskeep-expense-tracking

    Logs, lists, and summarizes job-tagged expenses — materials, mileage, other costs — ready to fold into an invoice.

  • Breakout

    opskeep-escalate-to-owner

    Pauses an autonomous transaction and brings in the business owner, via live handoff or a notify-and-pause fallback.

  • Breakout

    opskeep-triggers

    Creates, inspects, and deletes event-triggered automations with explicit trigger proposal confirmation.

  • Security

    opskeep-elliot-design

    Designs or reassesses a risk-proportional Secure by Design baseline before project implementation, first commit, or a material architecture change.

  • Security

    opskeep-elliot-review

    Reviews an already-existing local repository and produces an evidence-backed security assessment, visual dashboard, and prioritized recommendations.

Companion server

The judgment lives in the skill. The doing lives in the MCP server.

opskeep-tools and the breakout skills it routes to call out to a companion MCP server for the stateful half of the work: sending reminders, tracking time and expenses, recording escalations.
