# Opskeep Keep Clients — Sole Trader Note

Use when the requester is a solo tradesperson or solo freelancer whose repeat business
comes from referrals and repeat calls, not renewal contracts. See
[`docs/sole-trader-profile.md`](../../../docs/sole-trader-profile.md) for the full profile.

## What's different from the default profile

- Referral tracking matters more than renewal tracking. Most trades don't have a contract
  that renews; they get called again, or a client refers a neighbor. Treat "who referred
  this job" and "who might refer the next one" as first-class signals.
- Testimonials and reviews carry more weight for a sole trader than for an agency with an
  existing pipeline — often the primary source of new work. Prompting a happy client for a
  review right after job completion is a normal, expected touch, not an imposition.
- "Client health" for a one-off job is usually just: were they satisfied, would they call
  again, would they refer. There's no ongoing account to monitor between jobs.
- Renewal-specific workflow (contract terms, upsell cadence) rarely applies; don't force
  it onto a relationship that's actually job-by-job.

## Output adjustment

For a completed job:

- Satisfaction signal from the walkthrough/close-out.
- Referral source for this job, if known, and a note if a thank-you/referral ask is due.
- Review/testimonial ask, if the job went well and one hasn't been requested yet.
- Repeat-work opportunity (seasonal maintenance, related job) if there's a genuine, evidenced reason
  to expect one — not a manufactured upsell.
