# Video production gate checklist

Production: ____________________  Plan version: __________  Owner: ____________________

## Reference and brief

- [ ] Objective, audience, channel, aspect ratio, duration, CTA, and deliverables are recorded.
- [ ] Every reference is marked `verified`, `partial`, or `unverified` with honest access evidence.
- [ ] Verified references have a timestamped scene table spanning the relevant duration.
- [ ] Rights, licences, supplied assets, and unknown provenance are recorded without guessing.

## Direction and plan

- [ ] Fidelity invariants, adaptations, exclusions, and measurable tolerances are explicit.
- [ ] Every scene is classified as `reuse`, `extend`, or `invent` with a source or reason.
- [ ] Storyboard, timing, typography, transitions, audio cues, and acceptance checks are implementation-ready.
- [ ] The production plan validates with no errors.
- [ ] The creative director explicitly approved the named plan version; the exact quote is stored.

## Render review

- [ ] The exported render—not only source files, stills, or an agent report—was inspected.
- [ ] Timestamped observations cover fidelity, motion, composition, pacing, and polish.
- [ ] The weighted score and dimension floors pass, or a named exception records criterion and risk.
- [ ] Blocking defects and required revisions are resolved in a newer review version.
- [ ] The creative director explicitly approved the named render.

## Delivery

- [ ] `video_gate.py verify` passes against unchanged state, plan, and review evidence.
- [ ] Master and requested variants match format, duration, aspect ratio, audio, captions, and safe-area requirements.
- [ ] Final handoff names the approved version, score, exceptions, files, owner, and next action.
- [ ] No claim of reference fidelity exceeds the stored evidence.
