# Win the work

Covers everything before a client is signed: turning interest into a qualified
opportunity and keeping the pipeline honest.

## When to trigger

- "Someone reached out about [project]. What should I do next?"
- "Draft a follow-up to the lead from [source]."
- "What's stalling in my pipeline right now?"
- Requests to qualify, log, or prioritize a new opportunity.

## What to gather

- Source of the lead (referral, inbound, outreach, content): affects how it's qualified.
- What the prospect actually said, verbatim where possible; don't paraphrase away signal.
- Any existing pipeline record for this contact or project.

## What to produce

- A pipeline entry (or update to one) with: contact, source, stage, next action, and date.
- If asked to qualify: a short read on fit and a recommended next step, not just "looks
  promising," but a concrete "send a scoping call invite by Friday" or "pass, budget
  mismatch."
- Draft outreach/follow-up copy when asked, in the user's voice where a sample exists.

## Handoffs

- Once an opportunity is verbally agreed and ready to be scoped → `opskeep-scope-work`.
- If a qualified lead is actually a past client returning → `opskeep-keep-clients` may have
  relevant history to pull in first.

## Guardrails

- Never mark a lead "qualified" or "won" without an explicit signal from the user or the
  conversation record; flag uncertainty instead of rounding up.
